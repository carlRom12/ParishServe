Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Sacrament of Confirmation?

Confirmation is one of the three Sacraments of Christian Initiation. Through it, the grace received at Baptism is strengthened and sealed by the gift of the Holy Spirit.

Who can receive Confirmation?

Baptized Catholics who have reached the age of discernment may receive Confirmation. Please confirm the current age requirement with the Parish Office.

What are the requirements for Confirmation?

Requirements may vary. Please refer to the latest requirements provided by the Parish Office.

Do I need to attend seminars or catechesis sessions?

Candidates are generally expected to complete catechesis sessions before Confirmation. Please confirm the current schedule with the Parish Office.

Can non-parishioners apply for Confirmation?

Please coordinate with the Parish Office regarding requirements for non-parishioners.

Is there a fee for Confirmation?

Please confirm the current fee, if any, with the Parish Office.
