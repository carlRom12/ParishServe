About the SacramentThe meaning and grace of Confirmation.

Confirmation strengthens the grace you received at Baptism and seals it with the gift of the Holy Spirit. It is one of the three Sacraments of Christian Initiation.

Prepare. Grow. Be Confirmed.

The Sacrament of Confirmation strengthens your faith and calls you to be a witness of Christ in the world.

“For you did not receive a spirit of slavery to fall back into fear, but you have received a spirit of adoption as sons, by whom we cry, ‘Abba! Father!’”

— Romans 8:15
EligibilityWho can receive Confirmation and how to apply.

Who may receive Confirmation? Baptized Catholics who have reached the age of discernment.

Please contact the Parish Office to confirm the current age requirement.

From another parish? Please contact the Parish Office about the requirements for non-parishioners.

Requirements & PreparationDocuments and catechesis to prepare.

Prepare your documents. Requirements may vary. Check the latest list from the Parish Office before you apply.

Prepare for the sacrament. Candidates are generally expected to complete catechesis sessions before Confirmation. Ask the Parish Office for the current session schedule.
