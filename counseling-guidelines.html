Counseling Guidelines

To ensure a meaningful and respectful experience for everyone, please take note of the following guidelines before requesting a counseling session.

General Guidelines
  • Treat your counselor and fellow parishioners with kindness and respect.
  • Share your concerns at your own pace and provide accurate contact information.
  • Parish counseling offers pastoral support, prayer, and spiritual guidance.
Scheduling Guidelines
  • Request a session through the parish office and wait for confirmation.
  • Sessions are subject to the availability of the parish priest or designated pastoral counselor.
  • Arrive on time. If you need to cancel or reschedule, inform the parish office as early as possible.
During the Session
  • Help maintain a calm, respectful environment and silence your phone.
  • Speak openly about the support you are seeking, while respecting your own comfort and boundaries.
  • Discuss any follow-up arrangements with your counselor before leaving.
Confidentiality and Privacy
  • Discuss confidentiality and its limits with your counselor before sharing sensitive information.
  • Respect the privacy of others and share only information needed for your session.
  • Do not photograph or record a session without everyone's consent.
Important Reminders
  • Session arrangements and availability should be confirmed with the parish office.
  • Pastoral counseling provides spiritual support; it does not replace professional medical or mental health care.
  • For a life-threatening emergency, contact the appropriate emergency services.

A Shared Responsibility

These guidelines help us maintain a respectful, prayerful, and supportive environment for everyone seeking guidance.

“Be kind and compassionate to one another.”— Ephesians 4:32
