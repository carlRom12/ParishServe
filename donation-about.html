About Donations

Your generosity helps sustain the mission of the parish and support those in need.

“God loves a cheerful giver.”— 2 Corinthians 9:7
About DonationsGuidelinesHow to DonateGive Now

Why Donations Matter

Parish donations help maintain our church, support our liturgical celebrations, assist our outreach programs, and sustain the many ministries that serve our parish community.

Through your generosity, we are able to keep our church a welcoming place of prayer, continue our mission of service, and reach out to those in need with Christ’s love.

Every gift, no matter the size, becomes a blessing to the community.

How Your Donation Helps

Church Maintenance

Helps care for parish facilities and sacred spaces.

Liturgical Needs

Supports Mass supplies, celebrations, and church activities.

Charitable Outreach

Assists community programs and those in need.

Parish Ministries

Strengthens formation, youth, and service ministries.

Ways to Support

Donations may be sent through the parish’s official GCash QR code or brought directly to the parish office. Please verify the official payment details with the parish before sending your offering.

Please use only the official donation channels announced by the parish.

A Gift from the Heart

Giving is an act of faith, trust, and love. Your donation helps build a stronger parish, supports those in need, and allows us to continue the work of Christ in our community.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does my donation help?

Donations support church maintenance, liturgical needs, outreach, and parish ministries.

Where can I get the official GCash QR code?

Please contact the parish office for its verified GCash QR. The official payment QR is not yet available on this website.

Can I give a custom amount?

Yes. You may choose the amount you would like to give.
