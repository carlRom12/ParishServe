What is Pastoral Counseling?

Parish counseling offers a welcoming and confidential space to find spiritual guidance, emotional support, and a listening heart. Rooted in our Catholic faith, it seeks to accompany individuals, couples, and families through life’s challenges with compassion, prayer, and hope.

Our parish is here to walk with you — offering support, encouragement, and the grace of Christ as you seek healing, clarity, and peace.

How Counseling Can Help

Spiritual Guidance

Find clarity and direction through faith and prayer.

Emotional Support

Receive a listening heart and compassionate support.

Prayer & Reflection

Be accompanied in prayer during life’s challenges.

Family or Personal Concerns

Support for individuals, couples, parents, youth, and families.

Who May Request Counseling?

Parishioners, as well as individuals, couples, parents, youth, and families, may request counseling. Sessions are subject to parish availability and are facilitated by our parish priest or designated pastoral counselors.

Additional Notes

Details regarding counseling sessions, availability, and process may vary by parish. If you are experiencing a life-threatening emergency, please contact the appropriate emergency services.

A Safe and Compassionate Space

You are welcome here. Whatever you are going through, you will be heard with respect, without judgment, and in confidence. Our parish is committed to walking with you in faith, hope, and love.
