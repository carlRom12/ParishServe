About Counseling

Parish counseling provides a respectful and confidential ministry for individuals and families seeking spiritual guidance, emotional support, and a listening presence. Our priests and pastoral team are here to walk with you in your journey, offering prayer, encouragement, and hope through faith.

“Cast all your anxiety on Him
because He cares for you.”— 1 Peter 5:7
