“God loves a cheerful giver.”— 2 Corinthians 9:7
About DonationsGuidelinesHow to DonateGive Now

Donation Process

Make a donation to support the mission and ministries of our parish in four simple steps.

  1. 1

    Enter Donation Details

    Choose your purpose and enter a custom amount.

  2. 2

    Scan GCash QR

    Use the verified parish GCash QR to send your donation.

  3. 3

    Upload Proof of Payment

    Attach a clear screenshot or receipt reference and submit.

  4. 4

    Wait for Confirmation

    Parish staff review and confirm the donation.

Step-by-Step Guide

Follow these detailed steps to complete your donation.

  1. 1

    Choose the donation purpose.

    Select the area you wish to support, such as church maintenance, ministries, or outreach.

  2. 2

    Enter your custom donation amount.

    Type in the amount you want to give.

  3. 3

    Scan the parish GCash QR code and complete payment.

    Open Give Now for payment details. Use only the verified parish QR; contact the parish office if it is not available.

  4. 4

    Upload the proof of payment and submit.

    Attach a clear screenshot or receipt reference so the parish can verify your donation.

Please double-check the amount and recipient account before sending your donation.

Quick Reminders

  • Donations are voluntary.
  • Use the official parish GCash QR only.
  • Keep your screenshot or receipt for reference.
  • Confirmation may take some time depending on office hours.
