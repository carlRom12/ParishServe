About Mass IntentionsRemember loved ones and pray for a particular need.

A Mass Intention is a special way to remember loved ones or pray for a particular need. The priest offers the Mass for that intention, joining your prayer with the sacrifice of Christ on the altar.

Remember loved ones.
Give thanks. Pray with hope.

How It WorksFrom your intention details to parish confirmation.

Your Mass Intention request will go through three simple steps:

  1. 1

    Intent Details

    Provide your intention and contact information.

  2. 2

    Schedule

    Choose your preferred Mass date.

  3. 3

    Review & Submit

    Confirm your request before sending.

The parish will review your request and contact you once it is confirmed.

Reminders & OfferingImportant details and the standard Mass offering.
  • Mass intentions are offered in accordance with Catholic Church teachings.
  • Please provide accurate information.
  • The standard offering is ₱100.00. For the Souls of: maximum 2 names, at ₱100 per soul (₱200 for two).
  • Requests are subject to parish review and schedule availability.

For questions about schedules or accepted intentions, please contact the parish office.
