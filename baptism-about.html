What is Baptism?

Baptism is the first sacrament of Christian initiation. It cleanses a person from original sin, welcomes them into the Church, and begins a new life in Christ. Through Baptism, we become children of God and members of His family, called to live the Christian life.

Baptism is the gateway to life in the Spirit.

Importance of Baptism

Cleanses from Sin

Baptism frees us from original sin and gives us new life in Christ.

Welcomes into the Church

Through Baptism, we are received into the family of God, the Church.

Begins Christian Life

Baptism marks the beginning of our journey of faith and discipleship.

Calls Families to Faith

Baptism invites parents, godparents, and the whole family to live and share the faith.

Who Can Receive Baptism?

Infants and unbaptized persons may receive Baptism. Usually, parents or guardians prepare the baptism request for infants, and godparents are chosen to support the individual’s faith journey and help them grow in the Christian life.

Specific parish practices, requirements, and schedules may vary. Please refer to parish guidelines or contact the parish office.

A Sacred Beginning

Baptism is the beginning of a new life in Christ — a gift of grace, a call to holiness, and an invitation to be part of the Church.

At Our Lady of the Gate Parish, we joyfully accompany families as they present their children for Baptism and welcome new members into our faith community.

Through this sacrament, we are united in Christ and empowered to live as His disciples in the world.
