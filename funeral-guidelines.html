About Funeral Services

A prayerful farewell rooted in faith, hope, and Christian love.

“Blessed are those who mourn,
for they shall be comforted.”— Matthew 5:4
About Funeral Services Requirements Schedule Request Service

What are Funeral Services?

The Catholic Church accompanies families through the passing of their loved ones by celebrating funeral Masses and prayers for the deceased. Through the Church’s liturgical rites, we commend the faithful departed to the mercy of God, offer spiritual support to the grieving, and provide a dignified Christian farewell grounded in faith, hope, and the promise of eternal life.

“Eternal rest grant
unto them, O Lord.”

— Traditional Prayer

How the Parish Accompanies Families

Funeral Mass

The parish may celebrate a Funeral Mass for the deceased.

Prayerful Support

The Church prays with the family in a time of grief.

Burial Coordination

The burial location may be different from where the Mass is celebrated.

Christian Farewell

Funeral rites honor the life of the departed with dignity and faith.

What Families Should Know

For now, the confirmed requirement is the Death Certificate. Families may request a Funeral Mass at Our Lady of the Gate Parish even if burial or interment will take place elsewhere. Final schedule and additional instructions are subject to parish review.

Additional requirements, fees, and schedule details may vary. Please refer to the latest instructions of the Parish Office.

A Ministry of Comfort

In times of loss, the parish walks with you in faith and prayer. Through the celebration of the funeral rites, we offer consolation, support, and hope in Christ’s promise of eternal life. You are not alone — our parish community is here to journey with you during this difficult time.
