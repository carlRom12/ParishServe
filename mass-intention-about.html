What is a Mass Intention?

A Mass Intention is a prayer request offered during the celebration of the Holy Mass for a specific person, whether living or deceased, or for a special need or intention.

Through the Mass, we unite our prayers with the perfect sacrifice of Christ, entrusting our loved ones and intentions to God’s mercy and love.

We unite our prayers
with the sacrifice of Christ.

Why Offer a Mass Intention?

For the Deceased

A spiritual act of charity to pray for the repose of their souls.

For the Living

A prayer of support, healing, and strength for loved ones.

Thanksgiving

A way to give thanks to God for His blessings in our lives.

Special Intentions

For personal needs, important occasions, or other intentions.

Who May Request One?

Parishioners and their families may request Mass intentions for the living or the deceased, as well as for special intentions. All requests are subject to the parish schedule and must be confirmed by the parish office.

Details, available schedules, and parish procedures for Mass intentions may vary. Please contact the parish office for the most accurate and up-to-date information.

A Prayer Offered with Faith

Every Mass is a powerful prayer. When you offer a Mass intention, you entrust your loved ones and needs to the Lord, joining them to the Eucharistic sacrifice. It is a beautiful way to express your faith, love, and communion with the Church.

Remember loved ones in prayer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request a Mass Intention?

Provide your intention and contact information, choose your preferred Mass schedule, and review your request using the Mass Intention request form.

Is my preferred schedule guaranteed?

Requests are subject to availability and confirmation by the parish office.

What is the Mass Intention offering?

The standard offering is ₱100.00 per intention. Contact the parish office for offering arrangements.
