Requirements

Prepare the documents and complete the parish steps for your church wedding request.

“What God has joined together, let no one separate.”— Mark 10:9
About WeddingRequirementsScheduleRequest
Steps to be TakenDocuments NeededFees & Parish PoliciesSponsors & Wedding Party

A. Steps to be Taken

1

Couples have to present themselves personally for interview by a priest, at least one month before the scheduled wedding. Earlier than one month is preferred.

2

Attend the Pre-Marriage seminar in the parish or in a parish convenient for them. If they decide to attend the pre-marriage seminar in another parish they will have to present a Certificate of Attendance duly signed by a priest from that parish.

3

Two days before the wedding or on the eve of the wedding couples should go to confession. The parish also recommends that all parties to the wedding go to confession (sponsors, parents, et al.).

4

On the day of the wedding, arrive on time so that you do not disrupt the succession of activities in the church.

B. Documents Needed

1.Certificate of No Marriage (CENOMAR) from the Statistics Office.

2.Permit for non-parishioners and outsider (BRIDE only).

3.Baptismal & Confirmation Certificates of the couples.

4.Publication of Banns in the Parish and in the parishes where the parties reside.

5.Marriage License

6.2 x 2 Picture

7.Authorization number of the Guest Priest (If a guest priest is engaged)

8.In case one or both of the couples is/are military person(s), a certification from his/her commanding officer to the effect that he/she is free to marry.

C. Fees / Decorations / Cancellations

Wedding Fees / Decorations
1 Ordinary Wedding (Thursdays) ₱5,000.00
2 Special weddings for Parishioners ₱12,000.00
3 Special weddings for non-parishioners belonging to the diocese ₱15,000.00
4 Special weddings for Outsider ₱20,000.00
5 Evening Wedding (6:00PM) – (Mon, Tue, Thu) ₱20,000.00

The fee includes the wedding fee, Mass stipend for the priest, and the choir.

Decorations, Reservations & Cancellations

  1. There is an in-house decorator which couples may avail depending on their taste. See the separate brochure and contact the Mother Butler’s Guild at their office.
  2. Decorators outside of the parish contracted by the couple should be coordinated with the parish. Outside decorators are required to pay Two Thousand Five Hundred Pesos (₱2,500.00) for the red carpet and janitorial fee because of the trash often left in the church.
  3. The couple is responsible for telling their decorators to remove their decorations immediately to give enough time for the succeeding wedding to set up.
  4. Date reservations are allowed. Couples will have to pay a ₱2,000 reservation fee, which is non-refundable if the wedding is cancelled or moved to another date.
  5. The parish requires full payment two weeks before the scheduled wedding date. Otherwise, the parish reserves the right to cancel the wedding and award the slot to another wedding.
  6. For sponsors in excess of six pairs, the couple will have to pay a donation for every extra pair of sponsors.
  7. When couples cancel their scheduled wedding for whatever reason, payments will not be refunded because their slot has already been reserved for that wedding.

D. Primary / Secondary Sponsors, Ring Bearers, Flower Girls, Guest Priests

1. Couples may have as many sponsors as they want, the parish recommends only a maximum of six pairs due to space constraints. However, in excess of six pair, couples will have to pay the fee as stated above.
2. There should be only three pairs of secondary sponsors i.e., candle, veil, cord.
3. Children acting as ring bearers, flower girls and others should be old enough to avoid misbehavior from very young kids and preserve the solemnity of the wedding. The parish requires children to be seven years old and above.
