Process Overview

Your wedding request will go through these simple steps:

  1. 1

    Prepare

    Read the guidelines and complete the requirements.

  2. 2

    Submit

    Send your request and documents online.

  3. 3

    Review

    Our parish staff will review and confirm your schedule.

You will be notified by email or text once your wedding schedule is confirmed.

Important Reminders

  • Apply at least 1 month before the preferred wedding date.
  • Attend the Pre-Marriage Seminar.
  • Submit complete and valid documents.
  • Arrive on time on your wedding day.
  • Coordinate with the parish office for any special arrangements.

Need Help?
