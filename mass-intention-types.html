Common Types of Mass Intentions

Here are the most common kinds of Mass intentions you may request. Each intention is a beautiful way to unite your prayers with the Holy Sacrifice of the Mass.

  • Thanksgiving Mass

    To thank God for blessings received, such as passing a board examination, recovery, a birthday, or an anniversary.

    Example: Thanksgiving for passing the board examination.

  • Special Intention

    For a particular personal, family, or community intention.

    Example: Guidance and unity for our family.

  • Petition Mass

    To ask for a grace or help needed, such as healing, strength, guidance, or help with an examination.

    Example: Healing and strength for Maria Santos.

  • All Souls

    A general intention for all the faithful departed. Individual names are not needed.

    Example: For all the faithful departed.

  • For the Souls of

    For one or two named deceased persons. Enter each full name separately. The offering is PHP 100 per soul: PHP 100 for one or PHP 200 for two.

    Example: For the souls of Juan Dela Cruz and Maria Santos.

All Mass Intention requests are subject to parish review and confirmation. Available schedules may vary depending on parish activities, liturgical reasons, or other circumstances.

Helpful Reminder

Mass intentions should be respectful and aligned with Catholic Church teachings. Keep your intentions focused on prayer, healing, thanksgiving, and other spiritual needs. For guidance about a particular request, please contact the parish office.
